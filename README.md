## Introduction

Curious about the time? Just look at the Sun!

Little Sunshine is a desktop sun clock designed for Apple Vision Pro.

## Key Features

- A small, quiet solar clock that fits perfectly on any corner of your desk;

- Experience the flow of time through the Sun’s position and effortlessly capture the beauty of day and night transitions;

- A character stands where you are on Earth, and the Sun rises and sets according to your time zone;

- Customize the time and character details to your liking;

- Anti-mistouch design ensures no disruption to your flow.
